Who’s coming to dinner?

Assign each slot to a Human or CPU before starting. CPU turns play automatically; their answer buttons are locked. Humans share this screen and take turns. Classify one food, then choose Eat or Skip.

How to play this first version

Four meal rounds per day, plus a surprise snack. Eat a correctly identified healthy food for 1 point (up to three per day, plus the snack). One optional treat earns 0 points; eating a second eliminates you.

Daily wrong-answer limits: Easy 3, Medium 2, Hard 1. Mistakes reset each new day. Wrong answers and ordinary skips end your turn. Snack time has no timer. Correctly label an unhealthy snack and get another food. Once you correctly label a healthy snack, Eat it to stay in; Skip eliminates you. Daily mistake limits still apply.

Baby, Child, and Adult change the food questions. Hard also spins the meal wheel. Highest surviving score wins; ties share the win.

These are simplified game categories for specific foods and portions. Foods are not moral choices, and this game is not a real-life meal plan.
