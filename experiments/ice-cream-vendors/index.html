The broader idea

What carries beyond the beach?

A choice can make sense given what you know and still lead to a different result. Other people are making choices too; the outcome alone does not tell you whether your decision was bad.

Here, every vendor wants more customers. Outside this model, even that goal may be uncertain. Before predicting someone’s next move, ask what they are trying to achieve.

How do B and C choose?

Each independently chooses the position that would have served them best if the others had stayed in yesterday’s positions. They do not see your new choice or each other’s new choice first. This is one deliberately simple strategy, not a claim about real vendors.
