What Is Worth Learning?

What uncertainty could change this decision, and what is the cheapest credible way to reduce it?

Use this when the need for more information is expanding into research without a clear stopping point.

You’ll be able to choose an unanswered question whose answer could change your decision.

The idea

Starting from a specific decision can narrow a broad information problem. Identify the uncertainty that could change the choice, then work backward to the evidence needed and a credible way to obtain it.

Worked example · illustrative

Which question should the interviews answer?

Situation. A community group is considering moving its weekly class online. Members propose a broad survey about everyone’s technology habits. The immediate decision is whether regular attendees could participate from home.

The tempting conclusion. Collect as much information as possible before choosing a format.

What changes the view. The organizer identifies the assumption behind the move: attendees have a suitable device, a connection, and a place to join. A few targeted conversations followed by a short practice session can test that assumption more directly than a long general survey. The practice session is useful because reported confidence alone may not reveal access difficulties.

What this supports. Gather evidence about the uncertainty that could change the format decision. If access is a barrier, consider an in-person option before investigating unrelated preferences. Start small, but include people likely to face difficulties; a convenient sample of confident users would not resolve the question.

Useful when

Planning customer interviewsTesting whether an assumption generalizesScoping research before a design decisionChoosing the next experimentDeciding whether more investigation would be useful

Watch for

Not all useful discovery begins with a known decision. Use this lens for focused investigation without eliminating exploratory learning. Refining the question also has a cost; seek evidence when further analysis is unlikely to help.
