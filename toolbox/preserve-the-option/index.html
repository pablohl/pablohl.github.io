Preserve the Option

What does this decision make possible or impossible afterward?

The idea

Immediate value isn't the only consequence of a choice. Some decisions alter the future option set and the cost of responding to new information.

Situation

One option is better now, but choices may constrain future responses.

Useful when

Architecturecustomer commitmentspricingsecurityintegrationsdifficult-to-reverse choices

Watch for

Preserving optionality has a cost. Don't spend heavily protecting against every imaginable future.

Where this came from

Startups, Research and development, Star Trek

Lineage
