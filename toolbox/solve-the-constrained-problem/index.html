Solve the Constrained Problem

What solution works under the constraints that actually exist?

The idea

Real problems are defined partly by deadlines, latency, resources, incomplete data and other constraints. A theoretically imperfect approximation may solve the actual problem better.

Situation

The theoretically correct solution is expensive, slow or incompatible with real constraints.

Useful when

Engineering trade-offsdeadlinesprototypesresource constraints

Watch for

“Pragmatic” shouldn't become an excuse for ignoring requirements that genuinely determine success. Sometimes complexity really is necessary.

Pommerman, Competitions, Applied machine learning
