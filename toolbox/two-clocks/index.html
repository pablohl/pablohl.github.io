Two Clocks

What happens to the decision while I’m waiting?

The idea

Delaying a decision changes both what we know and what we can still do. Waiting is useful when the information we expect to gain justifies the costs and changes to our options.

Situation

Waiting may bring useful information, but the available options may change before it arrives.

Useful when

Waiting for customer requirementsDelaying a difficult-to-reverse commitmentDeciding whether to run another experimentActing before an opportunity expires

Watch for

Time alone does not produce useful information. The two clocks are a metaphor, not directly comparable rates; ask whether the expected learning could change the decision and justify the cost of waiting.

Where this came from

Lineage
